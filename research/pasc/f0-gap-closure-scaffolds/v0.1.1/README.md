## What this publication is

This technical note converts the six `NOT_SATISFIED` PASC F0 criteria into a dependency-aware work programme. It provides:

- an author-acceptance payload template;
- a canonical-source-record projection boundary;
- a candidate adapter-artifact plan;
- an independent-human-review disposition structure;
- a blind commit–reveal field replay protocol;
- a fail-closed protected-boundary preservation posture;
- a reserved-territory compatibility matrix;
- an ordered path for future gap-closure work.

## Design bridges

- **Explicit bridge:** PAMDC/PACR authority collapse → PASC exact negative admissibility → parent-owned re-entry or continued `HOLD`.
- **Quiet bridge 1:** A6 composition may constrain bounded offices but cannot manufacture successor identity or origin.
- **Quiet bridge 2:** Continuity Bundle / Continuity Metric remain parent-owned; archive, replay, persistence, or resemblance do not become PASC-native continuity proof.

## Engineering ground paragraph

After the responsible engineer disappears from a construction site, possession of the gate key, payment of the electricity bill, or custody of an old drawing does not authorize redesign. The safe first response is narrower: stop load-changing work, preserve measurements and drawings, maintain temporary supports, record every intervention, and wait for an independently qualified engineer with a valid mandate. This scaffold prepares the forms and checks for preserving that load path; it does not appoint the next engineer.
