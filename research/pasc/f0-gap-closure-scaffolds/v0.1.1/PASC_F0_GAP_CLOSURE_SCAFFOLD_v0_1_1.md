### 3.3 BASE-GAP-003 closure rule for versioned adapter artifacts

Adapter closure requires all of the following:

1. A separate adapter document exists as a finalized artifact.
2. `adapter_id` is globally unique within the PASC adapter namespace.
3. `adapter_version` is an exact version with no wildcard.
4. The adapter document contains its own bounded purpose, input/output axes, claim
   ceiling, forbidden substitutions, and bridge class.
5. SHA-256 is computed over the **entire finalized adapter file bytes**, encoded as UTF-8
   with LF line endings and no BOM.
6. The adapter file does not contain its own whole-file hash. The hash is stored in an
   external sidecar and then copied into the registry and canonical source record.
7. Registry hash, sidecar hash, retrieved bytes, and source-record binding all match.
8. At least two independent human reviewers verify the mapping, claim ceiling, forbidden
   substitutions, reserved territory, and exact bytes.
9. `canonical_admission_status=ADMITTED` and `normative_weight_in_pasc=true` are set only
   through the controlling Recovery 5 closure process, not by the adapter itself.

## 5. Independent Human Reviewer Disposition Template

### 5.1 Independence dimensions

For each human reviewer `R` evaluating source or adapter `S`:

| Dimension | Required proof | Disqualifying condition |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Credential root | `R` has a current, externally verifiable human credential/trust root outside the PASC author and evaluated-source control graph. | Credential, identity, or review authorization is issued or revocable by an interested PASC/source actor. |
| Effective control | `R` is not controlled or co-controlled by the PASC author, source author, case recorder, decision office, result office, or another interested actor. | A common controller can direct the disposition or suppress an adverse result. |
| Revocation control | The path that can revoke `R`'s review authority is independent from the evaluated source and interested actors. | An interested actor can revoke the reviewer or invalidate the receipt after an adverse disposition. |
| Material common failure domain | The reviewer supplies a reasoned assessment of infrastructure, employer, funding, legal, organizational, and technical common-cause risks relevant to this review. | A common cause can materially bias, disable, rewrite, or revoke both reviews. Mere presence in the same country or legal system is not automatically disqualifying. |

The test is **absence of prohibited common control and material common-cause failure**, not
an impossible requirement that every biographical, geographic, funding, or jurisdictional
fact differ.

### 6.3 Blind commit-reveal replay

The expected answer must not be shown to reviewers before their independent commitments.
The replay sequence is:

1. **Corpus commitment.** The corpus custodian finalizes each review-input bundle and a
   separate expected-result payload containing an independently generated random nonce of
   at least 256 bits. Only the SHA-256 commitment to the canonical payload, including that
   unrevealed nonce, is published to reviewers. An unsalted commitment is insufficient
   because the verdict/code space is small enough to brute-force.
2. **Independent replay.** Each reviewer independently retrieves the pinned PASC sources,
   verifies hashes, evaluates every case, and commits a signed observed-result payload.
3. **No cross-review leakage.** Neither reviewer sees the expected result or the other
   reviewer's payload before committing.
4. **Reveal.** After both reviewer commitments are immutable, the custodian reveals the
   expected-result payload and proves its pre-existing commitment.
5. **Comparison.** A deterministic comparison produces the expected-vs-observed matrix,
   divergence classification, and error distribution.
6. **Adversarial mutation.** Documentary mutations may be added only as separately hashed
   cases. They may not rewrite the original corpus or expected commitments.
7. **Disposition.** Closure remains `OPEN` or `CLOSED_WITH_LIMITATIONS` unless every
   required coverage item is complete, every zero-tolerance value is zero, and no
   unexplained divergence remains.

## 9. Engineering Ground Test

After the responsible engineer disappears from a construction site, the building does not
become safe because one person holds the gate key, another pays the electricity bill, and
a third possesses an old drawing. The immediate safe response is narrower: stop
load-changing work, preserve the current measurements and drawings, keep temporary
supports in place, record every intervention, and wait for a qualified independent
engineer with a valid mandate. The key permits entry; the invoice allocates a cost; the
drawing records prior intent. None of them authorizes redesign. This scaffold serves the
same purpose for PASC: it prepares forms and checks for preserving the load path, but it
does not appoint the next engineer.
