# TAP Claim and Boundary Map v1.0

This map prevents category errors. It is a compact guide to existing boundaries, not a new ontology.

| Category | What may be claimed | What may not be inferred | Parent mechanism | Minimum evidence requirement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Session AI | A model or service answers within a bounded session. | Persistence, temporal presence, continuity, authority, or identity. | Session/runtime definition. | Session configuration or observable invocation boundary. |
| Persistent assistant | Context or memory remains available across interactions; it may qualify as TAP if bounded participation is demonstrated. | `c`, consciousness, personhood, legitimacy, or governance from memory alone. | TAP memory and persistence boundaries. | Persistence declaration, classified memory, and control route. |
| Workflow agent | An agent performs scoped tasks over a workflow and may be TAP when its role persists across time. | General authority, hidden delegation, or c-class status. | Tool privilege, agent inventory, AGL/ARL where applicable. | Role inventory, capability scope, budget, and logs. |
| Local cognitive node | Local infrastructure supports inference, memory, or orchestration close to a user or environment. | Sovereignty, safety, ownership, or `c` from locality. | Local Cognitive Infrastructure Boundary Profile. | Locality disclosure, custody boundary, and TAP-T05 claim control. |
| Agentic hive | Multiple agents, models, memories, and tools are coordinated across time. | Unity, identity, single authorship, or authority from coordination. | TAP multi-presence boundary, SER-FED, AGL/ARL where applicable. | Complete agent inventory, provenance, role boundaries, and hidden-agent test. |
| TAP | Sustained bounded AI participation across time. | Consciousness, personhood, sovereignty, AGI, safety, legitimacy, or `c` by default. | This profile owns the category definition. | Minimum TAP claim requirements and applicable `TAP-T01` through `TAP-T10` evidence. |
| TAP-L4 | A TAP architecture has evidence-bound cost, time, scarcity, irreversibility, or consequence controls. | Full L4 Witness conformance, c-class status, or moral standing. | L4 Reality Boundary and L4 Witness. | L4 budget, privileged-event witness, test, and review route. |
| `c` | A separately anchored, governed, L4-bound, witnessed, memory-disciplined, challengeable temporal presence. | Automatic personhood, consciousness, sovereignty, or authority beyond the parent corpus. | `c = a + b` and its parent profiles. | Separate c-class conformance evidence; TAP evidence alone is insufficient. |
| MOT-c | Historically formed and reflectively reviewable motivational structure inside a continuing c-class line. | TAP existence, consciousness, owned motive from persistence, or a replacement definition of TAP. | MOT-c v0.1, DOI `10.5281/zenodo.22060517`. | MOT-c-specific theoretical and future empirical evidence; conceptual material is not TAP implementation evidence. |

## Boundary invariant

```text
All valid c-class systems are Temporal AI Presences.
Not all Temporal AI Presences are c-class systems.
```
