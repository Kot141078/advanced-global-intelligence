# Temporal AI Presence Profile v1.0 — Interpretive Addendum A

**Endogenous Cognition and the c-Class Conformance Boundary**  
**Author:** Ivan Kotov  
**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Publication date:** 2026-11-16  
**Published technical note:** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24265163  
**Zenodo record:** https://zenodo.org/records/24265163  
**Supplements:** [Temporal AI Presence Profile v1.0](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22070960), without replacement or retroactive amendment.

The note identifies an ambiguity between TAP v1.0's abbreviated c-class descriptions and the wider `c = a + b` research corpus. It proposes endogenous, self-directed cognitive activity as **necessary for the assessment of an active c-line**. A recurring scheduler, task-executing agent, retrieval loop or persuasive declaration of an internal goal is not by itself evidence of such a line.

It distinguishes the technological substrate `b`, an anchored formation candidate, observed longitudinal cognitive development and eventual evidence-backed conformance. Independent implementations may qualify regardless of their software labels. Pause and cold wake require separate continuity assessments; maturity on activation and uninterrupted 24-hour computation are not required.

This is a **published proposed interpretive clarification**, not a complete c-class conformance standard, not adopted normative incorporation into TAP v1.0, and not experimental evidence of consciousness, same-c identity or a formation-specific effect. No implementation gates or permissions changed.
