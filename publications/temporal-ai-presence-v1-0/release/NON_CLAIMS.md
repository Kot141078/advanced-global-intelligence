# Non-Claims and Claim Ceiling

Temporal AI Presence means sustained bounded AI participation across time. It is an architectural and governance category, not a declaration of inner state, identity, status, or unlimited authority.

This package makes no claim of:

- consciousness or sentience;
- personhood, moral status, sovereignty, or legal status;
- universal safety or production readiness;
- full `c` conformance or `TAP-C`;
- full M4 pass (`M4_FULL_PASS=false`);
- full deployment conformance or full TAP-T03 verification;
- independent external replication;
- autonomous authority from persistence, capability, local hardware, memory, tone, usefulness, or multi-agent behavior;
- motivation from persistence, or TAP conformance from MOT-c;
- one Ester deployment defining Temporal AI Presence.

One local reference implementation candidate was tested against the TAP contract. Its local execution is not a universal result, a production deployment attestation, or public evidence of every implementation path.

All evidence statements are bounded by `TAP_PUBLIC_EVIDENCE_MATRIX_v1_0.csv`, `TAP_CODE_REFERENCES_v1_0.json`, and `TAP_T03_DEPLOYMENT_BOUNDARY_v1_0.md`.
