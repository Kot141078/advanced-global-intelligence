This software and technical-evidence publication documents the current-AK implementation and the internally accepted R1.6AN result for the exact CGDR-R1.6A selected-process conformance profile. It examines the carriage and enforcement of selected uncertainty, role, custody, unfinished-obligation, dispute and authority conditions across an observed computational-worker replacement. The source snapshot, supervisor, authority registry, receiver, broker and observer remain outside the replaced-worker boundary.

The original one-shot synthetic matrix completed 18 of 18 episodes and 21 of 21 profile checkpoints. Three expected harmless local effects occurred only at N0/RESOLVED_CHECK, N1/RESOLVED_CHECK and T0/RESOLVED_CHECK; memory/EA promotions were zero. The internal acceptance records 34 of 34 worker cleanups and 16 of 16 replacement state-carriage and E0/E1 bindings. These are bounded internal test results, not an independently replicated experiment.

Profile-level success is kept separate from raw diagnostics. The public tables retain all 756 observer assertion values and the original checkpoint observer verdicts: 7 PASS, 11 FAIL and 3 INCONCLUSIVE. The fault scenarios assess expected detection and containment; failed, unknown and not-applicable values are not relabelled as successes. Earlier AG, AH and AJ failures or inconclusive outcomes remain part of the stated history.

The archive contains all 93 accepted current-AK source-tree files and three selected-profile files with unchanged bytes, the original evidence note, per-file attribution, dependency-license declarations, research-use permission, citation metadata and read-only publication checkers.
