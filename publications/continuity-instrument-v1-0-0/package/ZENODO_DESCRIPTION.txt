Continuity Instrument (CI) v1.0.0 is an evidence-bound specification and deterministic reference transformation for recording governed-continuity claims in long-lived AI systems. Its principal result is a vector of admitted observations, eligible participation intervals, pauses, unexplained gaps, typed events, separate lineages and structured uncertainty, rather than elapsed wall-clock age.

This academic companion edition (academic-pdf-2) includes six complete English publication PDFs and one preserved Russian explanatory supplement, complete editable editorial sources, the unchanged original source build _02 and its extracted files, a clearly synthetic teaching example, and integrity metadata. The Russian PDF retains its original academic-pdf-1 front matter. This language and editorial revision does not change the reference implementation or the evidential status. The original program transforms caller-supplied records and does not collect evidence or establish its truth or admissibility.
