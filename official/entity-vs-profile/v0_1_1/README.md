## Why this exists

Persistent AI systems can be marketed as companions, assistants, AI twins, or entities. Behaviour alone cannot distinguish whether the system is internally rooted or externally controlled. The note proposes witness-root custody as the standards-facing criterion.

## Core criterion

```text
Internal closed-box a-side custody -> entity possible.
External custody, escrow, registry root, platform root, usable root copy, or override -> profile.
```

## Package summary

This technical note defines a custodial criterion for distinguishing a persistent digital entity from a persistent digital profile. The criterion is witness-root custody: a construction may be classified as an entity only when its witness root is generated and held inside its closed-box boundary under a-side lineage custody. External custody, escrow, registry authority, platform root, or override capability classifies the construction as a profile.
