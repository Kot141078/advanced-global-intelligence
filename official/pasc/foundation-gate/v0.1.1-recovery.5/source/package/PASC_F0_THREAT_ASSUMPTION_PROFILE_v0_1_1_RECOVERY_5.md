This profile states the assumptions under which a PASC property is expected to hold and
how each assumption can be falsified. Assumptions are not hidden proof.

Every fail behavior below is selected under Expected Result Contract §1.1 and assumes

## 1. Collusion ceiling

Every future conformance claim must state the maximum tolerated collusion, independent
credential domains, shared infrastructure assumptions, emergency authority, legal
interfaces, and recovery limits. “Independent reviewers” without a failure-domain model
is not a testable security claim.
