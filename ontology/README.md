## Status of the early axiom

The axiom remains historically important. Its durable current reading is that a human anchor may introduce a formative asymmetry consisting of lived consequence, finite biological history, responsibility, values, limits, and contact with irreversible reality.

The early axiom must not be used as a universal claim that:

- life or subjecthood is granted or denied by origin alone;
- `a` transfers a measurable metaphysical substance;
- all machine substrate is permanently incapable of forming motivation;
- self-preservation is a mandatory natural motive of `c`;
- anchoring alone proves consciousness, personality, legal status, or authority.

The original EN/RU files are not modified by this note. Historical availability does not imply unrestricted current primacy.
